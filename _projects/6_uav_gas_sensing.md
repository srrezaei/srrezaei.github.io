A collaboration on a **low-cost gas sensor platform light enough to fly on a UAV**, able to detect
toluene in aqueous headspace in real time.

The interesting constraint here is the payload budget. Environmental monitoring instruments are
usually designed without much regard for mass; making one a quadrotor can actually lift, while
keeping the detection fast enough to be useful in flight, is a different design problem.
