During an eight-month Mitacs Accelerate research internship at **Quanser**, I contributed to the
development and testing of the QDrone 2 — an indoor autonomous quadrotor used for research and
teaching.

Most of the work was the part that does not appear in papers: implementing control algorithms on
the actual vehicle, designing test procedures, and finding the places where a controller that
behaves perfectly in simulation does something unexpected in the air. That experience shapes how I
write controllers now — I assume the model is wrong somewhere and design for it.
